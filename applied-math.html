THINKING
ABOUT
APPLIED MATH?

If you like patterns, models, computation, proofs, networks, differential equations, or figuring out why complicated systems behave the way they do, you may already be thinking like an applied mathematician.

01 / WHAT IS APPLIED MATH?

MATH THAT
HAS SOMEWHERE
TO GO.

Applied mathematics is about using mathematical ideas to understand systems, make predictions, uncover structure, and solve problems.

Sometimes the problem comes from physics, biology, engineering, computing, economics, or data. Sometimes the mathematics becomes interesting enough that the original application is almost beside the point.

01

Modeling

Take a messy real-world system and turn it into mathematics you can analyze, test, and improve.

02

Computation

Build numerical methods, simulations, and algorithms for problems that do not have neat closed-form answers.

03

Dynamical Systems

Use differential equations to understand systems that evolve: populations, circuits, motion, interacting components, and more.

04

Discrete Structures

Study graphs, networks, combinatorics, algorithms, and the mathematics of systems made from connected pieces.

05

Randomness + Uncertainty

Work with probability, stochastic systems, uncertainty quantification, and models where the outcome is not predetermined.

06

Analysis + Optimization

Ask what solutions exist, how systems behave, and how to find the best possible choice under competing constraints.
